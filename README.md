# 📊 Google Play Store Data Analysis
Turning Raw App Data into Actionable Insights

Understanding what drives app success in the mobile ecosystem requires analyzing patterns in installs, ratings, and user engagement.

In this project, I performed an end-to-end data analysis of Google Play Store applications using Python and Power BI to uncover insights about app performance and category trends.

The analysis combines data cleaning, exploratory analysis, and dashboard development to transform raw data into actionable insights for developers and product teams.

Using Python for data analysis and Microsoft Power BI for visualization, I explored how different features influence app performance and user engagement.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The primary goal of this project was to analyze Google Play Store data and answer key analytical questions such as:

Which app categories dominate the Play Store?

Do higher reviews lead to more installs?

What is the relationship between ratings and installs?

How do free and paid apps differ in terms of popularity?

## 📂 Dataset Overview
The dataset contains information about thousands of apps available on the Play Store, including:

- App Name

- Category

- Rating

- Reviews

- Installs

- Price

- Content Rating

- Genres

---

This dataset required extensive preprocessing due to inconsistencies in installs, missing ratings, and mixed data formats.

## 🧹 Data Cleaning Process

Key preprocessing steps included:

- Handling missing values in rating columns

- Converting installs column into numeric values

- Cleaning price column and removing special characters

- Removing duplicate applications

- Standardizing categorical variables

- These steps ensured reliable analysis and accurate insights.

---

## 📊 Key Insights from the Analysis

✔ Family and Game categories dominate the Play Store in terms of number of apps.

✔ Free apps significantly outnumber paid apps, indicating a freemium-driven ecosystem.

✔ Apps with higher review counts tend to achieve more installs, suggesting strong social proof.

✔ Most apps maintain ratings between 4.0 and 4.5, highlighting high competition for user satisfaction.

## 📈 Dashboard Visualization

---

## 💡 Business Insights

From a product or market perspective:

- App developers should prioritize user experience and reviews to increase installs.

- Entering oversaturated categories like Games requires strong differentiation.

- The Play Store ecosystem is highly driven by free applications with monetization strategies such as ads or in-app purchases.
